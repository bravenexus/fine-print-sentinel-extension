🛡 Welcome to Fine Print Sentinel

Your Terms & Privacy scanner. Six short things, then you're good to go.

We're moving fast into a world where not every company you sign up with has your wellbeing built into their terms. Fine Print Sentinel is on-the-go support: it scans so you don't have to manually copy, paste, and comb through every Terms of Service and Privacy Policy yourself.

Free, made by one person

Built for myself, shared in case it helps you too. No account, no cost, no catch.

Word-matching, not AI

Fine Print Sentinel checks a page's text against phrases I've flagged as worth a second look. No AI model involved.

Private by design

Everything runs locally in your browser. No server, nothing you scan is stored or sent anywhere, except the normal request to load the terms page itself.

Suggestions, not a legal opinion

Every flag means "go read that sentence yourself," not a verdict. It speeds up your research, it doesn't replace your judgment.

It will miss things, and occasionally overreact

A site can phrase something it doesn't catch, or it can flag something that's actually standard. Use your own judgment on every result.

Use it at your own risk

Offered as-is, no guarantee it's complete or accurate. By using it, you agree you're making your own decisions, not relying on it alone.

Unlike some of the sites this tool checks, nothing here is hidden or required, this page isn't blocking you from using the extension, it's just here so you know what you're using before you rely on it.
